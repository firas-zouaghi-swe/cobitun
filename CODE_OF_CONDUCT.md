# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Violence, threats, or discriminatory language
- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments
- Public or private harassment of any project member

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, block users, or take other actions to keep the community safe.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers.
